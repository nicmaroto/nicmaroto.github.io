UNB FENCING CLUB · 2026 - PRESENT

Student Executive Chair

I independently rebuilt the club’s student-executive model when its inherited governance no longer matched how the organization actually operated. The structure I designed replaces vague roles and overlapping authority with defined responsibilities, clear decisions and accountability for five officers.

I also created procedures for appointments, performance concerns, committee authority, mentorship and officer handovers so leadership does not have to start from zero every academic year. As Student Executive Chair, I coordinate the five officers, set priorities, chair meetings and oversee cross-team work. Both UNB Fencing and UNB Robotics adopted my executive model.

01 / STRUCTURE

I wrote the Student Executive Structure to resolve unclear reporting lines and the mismatch between outdated club rules and how the club now operates. It separates the Director’s authority from student responsibilities, assigns five specialized officer portfolios, and sets procedures for decisions, disagreements, appointments and performance issues.

The model also addresses knowledge lost during student turnover through ongoing officer records, succession and handover procedures, and a non-governing mentorship network. I authored the adopted governance document and committee guides to give future teams a usable framework, not just a list of titles.

02 / ACCESS

I initiated the Accessibility Committee and wrote its adopted operational guide, defining its purpose, responsibilities, reporting relationship, and safeguards around volunteers, privacy, approvals and external collaboration.

03 / DEVELOPMENT

I founded the Developmental Tournament Committee to create opportunities for members to practise refereeing, competition and event operations in a lower-pressure environment.

I authored the adopted operating guide, balancing collaborative event decisions with defined strategic authority, clear appointments, volunteer participation and operational safeguards.

04 / DUEL OF THE HILL
