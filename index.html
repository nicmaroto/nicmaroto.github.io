DESIGN & PROTOTYPING 2024

Prosthetic Arm Prototype

At UNB, our four-person team set out to design and build a prosthetic arm around a patient's particular needs. The challenge was about more than making a mechanism move: it meant translating a human requirement into a physical engineering solution.

It was an early opportunity to work through the full team design process - balancing ideas, feasibility, assembly, and a prototype that could be demonstrated.

Autonomous Piano

For a Yamaha Music showcase at FCS Innovation Academy, our three-person team built a robot that could play a piano autonomously.

My role centred on keeping the build moving: planning and preparation, coordinating assembly and tasks, and working through problems when the design needed adjusting.

ELECTRO-MECHANICAL DESIGN 2026

Guitar Distortion Pedal

Designing a RAT-inspired guitar distortion pedal in EasyEDA, from its 9 V power and bias network through an OP07 amplifier, adjustable gain, diode clipping, tone shaping, and output level.

Circuit simulations helped me examine how the gain and clipping stages change the signal. On the mechanical side, I'm applying CAD to the enclosure, control layout, and practical assembly. The full circuit and physical build are still in development.

01 / STRUCTURE

A student executive with real ownership.

I wrote the Student Executive Structure to define the Chair's coordination role, decision rights, meetings, and recordkeeping. The five officer portfolios cover Public Relations, Communications, Resources, Operations & Logistics, and Competition & Development.

It also defines officer terms, succession, mentorship, and living handover documents. Designed for UNB Fencing, the model was also taken up by the UNB Robotics Club.

02 / ACCESS

Accessibility as an ongoing responsibility.

I founded the Accessibility Committee and developed its operational guide so accessibility work has a defined place in the club, rather than depending on occasional individual effort.

The committee is part of a wider approach to making participation and organization more intentional.

03 / DEVELOPMENT

Making room to learn by doing.

I founded the Developmental Tournament Committee to prepare internal fencing competitions that give members chances to compete and learn refereeing and tournament operations.

Its operating model gives volunteers an equal voice on day-to-day event decisions while keeping strategic responsibility with the Chair. The tournaments are a developing initiative, not a completed event claim.
